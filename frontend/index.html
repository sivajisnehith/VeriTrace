Digital evidence is easy to copy.
Harder to prove.

Images and content spread across the web get reposted, cropped, re-encoded, and stripped of context. By the time evidence matters, it's rarely clear what was actually there, or when.

VeriTrace helps investigators discover matching evidence across the web and create a cryptographically verifiable record of exactly what was observed, and at what moment.

How VeriTrace works

Six stages carry a reference image from first identification to a verifiable, tamper-evident record.

  1. 01

    Identify

    Detect and analyze faces within a reference image.

  2. 02

    Discover

    Search the web for potentially matching evidence.

  3. 03

    Compare

    Rank candidate evidence by face similarity.

  4. 04

    Fingerprint

    Generate a deterministic SHA-256 fingerprint of the selected evidence.

  5. 05

    Anchor

    Register the fingerprint on the Ethereum blockchain.

  6. 06

    Verify

    Detect whether the registered evidence has since changed.

A fingerprint, not a currency

VeriTrace uses Ethereum for one purpose: giving a piece of evidence a permanent, tamper-evident identity. The fingerprint itself carries no value and represents nothing financial. It is simply a record that this exact evidence existed at this exact time.

Currently deployed on Ethereum Sepolia, a public test network.
